# Demo voice-over script — app_walkthrough.mp4

Voice: Microsoft Edge neural TTS, `en-US-AndrewNeural` (calm, professional, neutral American English).
Recorded against the real application UI, project "Gouda SHelf life", paper *CHE 0002 — Bee venom: A
potential natural alternative to conventional preservatives for prolonging the shelf-life of soft
cheese 'Talaga'* (Heliyon, 2024). No fabricated data — every screen, figure, table and value shown is
what this paper actually produced.

Each block below is one continuous scene in the final video. Timing is driven by the measured length
of its own narration clip (see `scene_durations.json` generated alongside the audio).

---

## Scene 1 — Project dashboard

Cheese Database turns scientific research papers into structured, reviewable research data.
Each project keeps its papers, extracted evidence and scientific results together in one workspace.

## Scene 2 — Open a real research paper

After uploading a research paper, the platform analyzes the document while keeping the original
paper available for inspection.

## Scene 3 — Evidence

Tables, figures and relevant scientific evidence are captured directly from the paper, while
preserving their connection to the original source.

## Scene 4 — Structured extraction

The extraction layer converts this evidence into structured scientific information, including
experimental conditions, treatments and reported measurements.

## Scene 5 — Review

Researchers remain in control. Extracted observations can be inspected, edited, approved or
rejected before they become trusted data.

## Scene 6 — Research Structure

The platform also preserves the scientific structure behind the measurements, connecting each
study to its experiments, treatment arms and observations.

## Scene 7 — Scientific Database

Approved observations flow into a searchable scientific database. Only fields that are relevant
to the paper need to appear, avoiding unnecessarily sparse datasets.

## Scene 8 — Export

The resulting dataset can then be exported in common formats for further analysis, modelling or
research workflows.

## Scene 9 — Closing

From the original research paper to structured, traceable data, Cheese Database keeps the
evidence, scientific context and researcher review connected throughout the workflow. Built for
research that needs to remain inspectable, structured and reusable.
